How it works

  1. 1

    Buy tickets at the booth

    Families pay with cash or card as usual. The booth taps the amount and scans the family's phone, and the tickets appear straight away.

  2. 2

    Show your code at stalls

    The stall taps what's being bought and scans the code on the family's phone. A big green tick means it's paid.

  3. 3

    Watch your tickets

    Every family sees how many tickets they have left and where they spent them, so they know when to grab more.

Why is it free?

Fair Tickets is free software. Every ticket is kept on the XDC Network, a public ledger that runs around the clock, so there are no servers for us to rent, no accounts and no subscriptions. The only thing you add is a little XDC to cover network fees, usually a few dollars' worth for a whole fair, and most of it comes back to you at the end of the day.

  • Free software: no subscriptions, no per-ticket fees, no sign-ups
  • Works on any iPhone or Android phone with a browser
  • Families can add it to their home screen in seconds
  • Houses or classes, a live tally screen and printable posters included
  • Reports and spreadsheet downloads for the treasurer

Questions

Do families need to download an app?

No. They open a link or scan a poster and tap Get started. They can add it to their home screen if they like.

Is my information private?

Yes. Fair Tickets never asks for names, emails or phone numbers. Each phone gets a random ticket code. The ledger shows ticket movements between codes, not people.

What if someone photographs my code?

The code changes every 30 seconds and each one works only once, so a photo is useless almost straight away.

What if a phone is lost?

When families start, they text themselves a backup message. Tapping its link on a new phone brings their tickets back.

Can children use their own phone?

Yes. A parent can text their backup message to their child's phone and open it there. Both phones then share the same tickets, and the parent sees everything spent.

What about mistakes?

Booths can undo a sale for a few minutes, stalls can refund their own charges, and the organiser can correct anything on the day.

Is it only for schools?

No. Churches, clubs, community groups, markets and businesses are all welcome to use it, free, for any event.

What do organisers need?

A phone, a little XDC for network fees, and helpers with smartphones for the ticket booth and stalls. The setup wizard walks you through everything.
